Workout Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026 | Publisher: Serdar Devir (Erdevir Labs)

Privacy Commitment: We respect your personal data. Workout Tracker is designed with an offline-first and privacy-focused architecture. We do not sell or monetize your private user data.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Local-First Storage: Any settings, cached content, or user preferences created in Workout Tracker are stored locally on your device using encrypted, sandboxed storage. We do not maintain external servers to monitor your personal activity.

Fitness and Health Data: Workout logs, step counters, and custom routines remain exclusively on your device. We do not transmit health logs to third-party ad networks.

2. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

If you purchase subscriptions or in-app items, payments are processed directly by Apple via the App Store (StoreKit). We never receive, process, or store your credit card or financial information. All transaction tokens and receipts are managed safely by Apple.

3. Analytics and Crash Reports

To improve stability and performance, the application may utilize anonymous crash diagnostics and standard performance metrics provided by Apple / system frameworks. These diagnostics are completely aggregated and never tied to your personal identity.

4. Children's Privacy

Our applications do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

5. Data Security

We employ industry-standard security safeguards to protect any data residing on your device. However, remember that no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. You are advised to review this page periodically for any updates. Changes are effective immediately upon posting to this URL.
